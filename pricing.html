Pricing FAQ

Can I try ViralClip for free?

Yes! The Starter plan is free forever with no credit card required. Pro and Growth plans include a 14-day free trial so you can explore all features risk-free.

What happens after my trial ends?

If you don't upgrade, you'll automatically move to the free Starter plan. No charges, no data loss. You can upgrade again anytime.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel from your dashboard at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do AI tokens cost extra?

Each plan includes a generous token allocation. If you need more, token packs start at $4.99 (500 tokens ≈ 25 clips). Pro and Growth plans also support BYOK — bring your own API key and pay AI providers directly at cost.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes — if you're not satisfied within the first 14 days, we'll issue a full refund. No questions asked.
